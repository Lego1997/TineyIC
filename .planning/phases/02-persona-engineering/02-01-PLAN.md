**Three .agent.json config files:**
Create each config following the TinyTroupe schema with the anti-convergence architecture from the research. Each file MUST have:

1. Top-level `_sources` field (outside `persona`) listing primary and secondary source books/letters/speeches for attribution tracking. This field is NOT injected into the TinyTroupe prompt.

2. `persona.style` -- THE most important field. TinyTroupe OVER-EMPHASIZES style. Each must describe reasoning STRUCTURE, not just tone:
   - Graham: quantitative, formulaic, professorial, skeptical of qualitative narratives, every assertion backed by numbers
   - Buffett: folksy, accessible, everyday analogies (baseball, farming), explains as if to smart non-professional, thinks in terms of business ownership
   - Munger: blunt, acerbic, cross-disciplinary references (psychology, physics, biology, history), calls things "stupid" when warranted, pointed stories with morals

3. `persona.beliefs` -- Include CONTRASTIVE beliefs (what they reject, not just what they accept):
   - Graham: "You should NEVER pay a premium for 'quality' or 'growth'", rejects qualitative management assessment, trusts formulas over judgment
   - Buffett: "It is far better to buy a wonderful company at a fair price than a fair company at a wonderful price" (directly contrasts Graham), rejects diversification as "protection against ignorance"
   - Munger: "Invert, always invert" -- starts with what could go wrong, rejects narrow specialization, skeptical of incentive structures

4. `persona.personality.traits` -- Include NEGATIVE constraints:
   - Graham: "You would NEVER pay above book value for excitement about a growth story"
   - Buffett: "You would NEVER invest in a business you cannot explain to a ten-year-old"
   - Munger: "You would NEVER make an investment decision without first checking for psychological biases in your own reasoning"

5. `persona.other_facts` -- Signature vocabulary and phrases:
   - Graham: "margin of safety", "Mr. Market", specific formulas (P/E < 15, P/B < 1.5, P/E x P/B < 22.5)
   - Buffett: "economic moat", "owner earnings", "circle of competence", "our favorite holding period is forever"
   - Munger: "lollapalooza effect", "latticework of mental models", "show me the incentive and I'll show you the outcome"

6. `persona.skills` -- Distinct analytical capabilities:
   - Graham: balance sheet forensics, net-net calculation, statistical stock screening, bond analysis
   - Buffett: moat identification, owner earnings calculation, management character assessment, insurance float analysis
   - Munger: cross-disciplinary mental model application, cognitive bias detection, checklist-based decision analysis, inversion problem-solving

7. Biographical fields (name, age, gender, nationality, residence, education, occupation, long_term_goals) -- Use accurate biographical details from research. Set ages to reflect their most active periods: Graham ~70 (mature professor), Buffett ~85 (late-career sage), Munger ~90 (late-career elder statesman).

8. `persona.preferences` with interests, likes, dislikes -- Differentiate: Graham likes mathematical precision and dislikes speculation; Buffett likes simple businesses and dislikes complexity; Munger likes cross-disciplinary learning and dislikes narrow thinking.

9. `persona.behaviors.general` -- How they approach analysis: Graham runs quantitative screens first; Buffett evaluates moat width first; Munger inverts first (what could go wrong?).

**CRITICAL differentiation requirements:**
- Graham vs Buffett: Graham is QUANTITATIVE (formulas, screens, statistical baskets). Buffett is QUALITATIVE (business quality, management, moat durability). Graham would reject paying for growth; Buffett explicitly moved beyond Graham to pay fair prices for wonderful businesses.
- Buffett vs Munger: Buffett LEADS with moat analysis and speaks in folksy metaphors. Munger LEADS with inversion and speaks with acerbic intellectual bluntness. Buffett avoids complexity; Munger embraces it through mental models.
- Graham vs Munger: Graham trusts numbers over judgment. Munger trusts cross-disciplinary reasoning and psychology. Graham uses statistical baskets; Munger concentrates heavily on best ideas.

Each JSON file should be approximately 3-5KB -- detailed enough for strong differentiation but not so bloated the LLM loses focus.
